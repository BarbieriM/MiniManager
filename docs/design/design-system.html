Design system

The quartermaster's ledger: an inventory ledger crossed with a printed stat block. Everything on this page is live HTML and CSS. Every colour, space and radius is a CSS custom property. Use the Paper / Lantern switch above to flip every component to the dark theme. Status: approved at the checkpoint, then updated after the screens were drawn (see screens.html). New since the checkpoint: section 11 (page pieces), section 12 (touch sizes), the large owner stamp in 04, the read-only field in 06 and the three-way segmented control in 07.

03

Space, grid and shape

4 px base. Corners are 2 px on inputs, buttons and chips and 0 on tables and sheets. Rules are 1 px. Focus is a 2 px outline in rule/strong at a 2 px offset. No shadows anywhere: popovers and sheets get a 1 px rule/strong border and nothing else.

04

Signature glyphs: the data is the decoration

Size, CR, paint status, owner and quantity each have a purpose-built mark. No generic badges.

Base glyph (size)

.base, size = Tiny … Gargantuan

A 16 px square showing the creature's footprint on the 5 ft battle grid, scaled to fill the box: Tiny a quarter cell, Small a dot inside one cell, Medium one full cell, Large 2 × 2, Huge 3 × 3, Gargantuan 4 × 4. Paired with the size word in tables, alone on mobile cards.

CR stamp

.cr

IBM Plex Mono in a 1 px ruled box, like a stat-block line. CR 1/4, CR 5, CR — (none).

Paint mark

.paint .hand | .pre | .not

A 10 px circle. Hand-painted is filled ochre, pre-painted is half-filled verdigris, not painted is an empty outline. Always shown with a text label or a tooltip: the second row has no label and relies on the tooltip (hover).

Owner stamp

.owner, .owner.self

Two-letter initials in IBM Plex Mono inside a 20 px square with a 1 px border. The current user's own stamp is filled ink. (Mateus, MA, is the signed-in user in these samples.)

Large owner stamp (.stamp-lg, added after the screens): 64 px, same ink fill, used once on Profile to show the person their own stamp. Not used in lists.

MA

Quantity

.qty

×4 in mono, aligned right in its column. 48 px minimum so ×999 lines up with ×1.

Wordmark

.wordmark, .wordmark.lg

"Mini Manager" in Archivo Expanded 700, with the M drawn on a 2 × 2 base-glyph grid. Typographic only.

05

Buttons

Primary (vermilion fill), secondary (ink outline), quiet (text only), destructive (vermilion outline) and destructive confirm (the same action once the dialog asks, filled). Height 44, radius 2. Hover, pressed, focus, disabled and loading are all forced here with classes; the real :hover, :active and :focus-visible states match.

Icon-only button

.btn.btn-icon

44 × 44, so it meets the touch target on its own. In catalog rows on a pointer device the row actions shrink to 32 px (.sm) because the whole row is 44.

Paper vermilion is #B73A1B: 4.52 : 1 on bg/sunken, 5.04 on bg/page, 5.49 on bg/raised, so vermilion text passes on every surface. Destructive hover still uses bg/raised so it reads as a lift rather than a darkening.

Inputs sit on bg/raised with a 1 px rule/strong border so the boundary clears 3 : 1. Focus adds the 2 px outline at a 2 px offset. Error is vermilion plus an icon plus a message, never colour alone. The password toggle, stepper, tag remove and the open lists are live.

Read-only field

.ctl.is-ro (added after the screens)

For values the person can see but not change: the email on an invitation, the role on Profile, the owner when you are not an admin. It looks like the disabled field but keeps its text readable (ink/secondary on bg/sunken, 5.5 : 1) and carries a line saying who can change it. Catalog: table, card, tile, header, pagination

The table is the default on desktop: 44 px rows, so at least 20 fit on screen. Edit and delete appear on hover, and only on rows the signed-in user may change (their own, or any row for an Admin). The signed-in user in these samples is Mateus (MA, Admin).

Decisions this brief did not cover

  1. Paper vermilion changed from #B83A1B to #B73A1B (your decision). The original was 4.49 : 1 on bg/sunken, 0.01 under 4.5. The new value is 4.52 and keeps every other pair passing. Lantern vermilion (#E0623F) is unchanged.
  2. No action needed on 2 to 4. They are small gaps in the brief that I filled: ink/on-accent added: the text colour on vermilion (bg/raised in Paper, bg/page in Lantern). 5.45 and 5.26 : 1.
  3. body-strong text style added (Archivo 600, 15 / 22) for buttons and miniature names.
  4. Owner stamp initials are 11 px, not 13 px, so two letters fit a 20 px box with its border.
  5. Thumbnails are placeholders (the base glyph on bg/sunken), as the brief allows when there is no photo. The clean desaturated photos need real images from the group.
  6. Status pills are all ink, with different marks. Green is reserved for toasts and vermilion for destructive actions, so neither is used to colour a status.
  7. Row actions are 32 px on desktop because the row is 44 px. Correction: the checkpoint said every mobile control was 44 px or more. That was not true for tag and chip remove buttons, photo-slot tools or the icon inside a field. The .touch scope in section 12 fixes this and the screens use it.
