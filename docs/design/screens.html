Screens, for reference

Every MVP screen at 390 and 1440, with its states, built from the approved design system and nothing else: same tokens, same components, same glyphs. These are static reference frames, not a clickable prototype: nothing here is wired together. Frames are named M / … and D / …. Mobile frames are 390 wide; list screens are cut at 844 (they scroll) and form screens are shown full length. Desktop frames are 1440 wide and shown at the scale chosen above. The Paper / Lantern switch flips every frame; the Dark check block pins Lantern on its own.

Sample data is the same everywhere: 312 minis, six owners (MA Mateus, AN Ana, RA Rafa, JU Júlia, CA Caio, BE Bea). The viewer is Mateus (MA) and an admin unless a frame says otherwise.
